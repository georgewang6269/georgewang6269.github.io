Step-by-Step Heron's Formula Solution

Mathematical Steps
Step 1
Calculate Semi-Perimeter ($s$)

Add all three sides and divide by 2:

$$s = \frac{a + b + c}{2} = \frac{3 + 4 + 5}{2} = 6$$
Step 2
Compute Terms $(s-a), (s-b), (s-c)$

Subtract each side length from the semi-perimeter:

$$(s-a) = 6 - 3 = 3$$ $$(s-b) = 6 - 4 = 2$$ $$(s-c) = 6 - 5 = 1$$
Step 3
Product of Terms

Multiply semi-perimeter by all difference terms:

$$\text{Product} = s \cdot (s-a) \cdot (s-b) \cdot (s-c) = 6 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 36$$
Step 4
Square Root (Final Area $\mathcal{A}$)

Take the square root of the product:

$$\mathcal{A} = \sqrt{36} = 6.00\text{ units}^2$$
